# Changelog

What changed in each version of Phase 10 Scorekeeper. The version number is shown at the bottom of the Menu. Dates are in Saskatchewan time.

## 24 (28 November 2026)

- Updating Phase 10 Scorekeeper no longer removes the offline copy of other NXLX apps opened in the same browser (such as Nightbloom). Before, someone with both apps on Android or a computer could find the other one would not open offline until it had been online once.
- This version changes the part that keeps the app working offline. Browsers pick that up on their own, but it keeps the app files you already have, so the app itself still only changes when you tap Update now.

## 23 (26 November 2026)

Accessibility, part 2:
- Each page (a round, Count, Menu, Players and the rest) works as a dialog for screen readers. While it is open, the screen behind it cannot be reached with Tab.
- Opening a page moves focus to its title. Closing it puts focus back on the button that opened it.
- The Escape key closes the top page, just like its Cancel, Back or Done button.
- After pressing Made phase, Went out, a seat arrow or another button, focus stays on that button instead of jumping to the top.
- Count: each + and − button says which cards it is for, and the new total is read out after each press.
- A player's warning is read with their points field. If Save stops, a screen reader hears what to check.
- The rounds tables have column headings, and the Σ row reads as Total. The card picture next to the dealer is skipped.
- The Update available bar is read out when it appears.
- In Players, the ↑, ↓ and ✕ buttons use the name as it is typed.

## 22 (26 November 2026)

Accessibility, part 1:
- Pinch to zoom works. Double-tap zoom stays off, so quick taps don't zoom.
- Text follows your device's text size setting (Larger Text on iPhone and iPad, and the browser's text size elsewhere).
- Clearer colours: dark text on the gold, coral and green buttons in dark mode, and a slightly deeper red and green in light mode. Field outlines are easier to see.
- Name fields and backup boxes have labels for screen readers. Made phase and the first dealer say when they are chosen, and the chosen dealer shows a ✓.
- Menu, Cancel, Save, Done, Edit a round and Update now are at least 44 px tall, and a clear ring shows keyboard focus.

## 21 (26 November 2026)

- The fade at the bottom of each page is now a slight 10 px feather.
- About: the install steps are a bulleted list, one device per line, with the device name in bold.

## 20 (26 November 2026)

- A lighter fade at the bottom of the Menu and the other pages. It now covers only the strip at the very bottom of the screen.

## 19 (26 November 2026)

- The Menu, Players, Past games and the other pages fade out softly at the bottom edge instead of being cut off, like the main screen.

## 18 (26 November 2026)

- More room above the title on iPhone when the app is opened from the home screen. The title sat in the soft blur under the clock.

## 17 (26 November 2026)

- Back up scores saves one file. Some phones also saved the share title as a separate text file beside the backup.

## 16 (24 November 2026)

- Links to the app show a preview picture on Reddit, Mastodon, Bluesky, Facebook and in chats.
- Clearer wording when a missed phase has too few points, for example "35 is too low for 10 cards".

## 15 (23 November 2026)

- Updates arrive only through Update now, never by themselves.
- Update now downloads every file before switching, so a dropped connection cannot leave the app unable to open.
- Two open copies of the app no longer save over each other's newer scores.
- A solid strip behind the Score round button, and the main screen holds still while another page is open.
- Big games (5 or 6 players) and long names fit the screen.

## 14 (23 November 2026)

- Blank points for a player who made their phase now warn instead of saving 0.
- A warning if the device stops saving scores.
- The Menu can restore the safety copy saved before the last update.

## 13 (23 November 2026)

- Renamed to Phase 10 Scorekeeper.
- Players can change seats at any time, even mid-game, and keep their points, phases and colour.

## 12 (22 November 2026)

- Install steps for every device: iPhone and iPad, Android, and computers.

## 11 (21 November 2026)

- An About page with the maker, support and Instagram links.

## 10 (21 November 2026)

- A player who missed their phase must have at least 10 cards (50 points), and points come in 5s. Warnings show as you type; you can still save anyway for house rules.

## 9 (21 November 2026)

- A new app icon.

## 8 (21 November 2026)

- First public version.
